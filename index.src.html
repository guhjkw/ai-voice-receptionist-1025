How it works

Four steps, one of them yours.

01

The call is answered

Your number forwards to Ava when you're busy, closed or don't pick up. She says she's an AI assistant and asks how to help.

02

It's triaged

Danger first (gas, CO, smoke), then flooding, then heat risk. Safety steps are read before anything else.

03

It's booked or handed over

Real times from the crew calendar, or a live transfer, on-call page or callback. Every call ends with a record and a summary.

04 · You

You set the rules

Your prices, hours, service area, on-call rota and what counts as an emergency. Ava answers only from them.

Honest scope. Copper Mesa Air & Plumbing is invented for this portfolio demo. Its prices, technicians, calendar, phone numbers and callers are invented, and no real customer data is shown. The understanding rules, emergency triage, calendar booking, guardrails and call records are real code that passes automated checks. No real calls, texts or pages go out. So the demo runs without an API key, Ava uses rules rather than a language model, and her voice is your browser's built-in voice. A delivered build runs on a voice platform (Vapi or Retell) with a natural voice and a language model (Claude) behind the same rules, on the client's own number, calendar and CRM, with access they provide during the build. If you use the microphone, your browser turns your speech into text (Chrome uses Google's speech service for this); this page stores nothing. No client build is shown or claimed.
